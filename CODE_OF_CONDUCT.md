# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ANIMATION is intended to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project. We expect everyone participating in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces to help maintain that environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Assume good intent and discuss technical disagreements constructively.
- Give useful, specific feedback rather than personal criticism.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and approaches.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Credit other creators when their work, ideas, or references influence a contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or deliberately demeaning language.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual content.
- Publishing private or sensitive information without permission.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberate abuse of project resources.
- Malicious code, intentionally harmful contributions, or attempts to compromise users.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation, or take other reasonable action when conduct violates this policy.

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers rather than escalating the situation publicly.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces and interactions conducted on behalf of ANIMATION, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and review conversations.

This document is adapted from common open-source community standards and may be updated as the project grows.
